About Us

🌞

Our Story

Founded in 2020, Kamado Kitchen was born from a deep love for Japanese culinary art. Our name "Kamado" — meaning traditional Japanese stove — reflects our commitment to time-honoured cooking techniques passed down through generations.

🍴

Our Philosophy

We believe in the beauty of simplicity. Every dish is prepared with the freshest seasonal ingredients, meticulous attention to detail, and a respect for the natural flavours that define Japanese cuisine.

🌿

Sustainability

We source locally whenever possible, partner with sustainable fisheries, and minimise food waste. Our goal is to nourish both our guests and the planet.
